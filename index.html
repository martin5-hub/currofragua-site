Curro Fragua auto-post

Internal publishing tool for the Curro Fragua music project.

What it is

Curro Fragua is an AI-assisted music project. We write, produce and publish original flamenco songs and their music videos.

Curro Fragua auto-post is the internal desktop tool we use to publish those finished videos to our own social accounts, including the artist's TikTok account @currofragua. It runs as a command-line program on the operator's own computer. It is not a product, it is not distributed, and it has no users other than the account owner.

How it works

The account owner authorizes their own TikTok account once, through TikTok's standard OAuth screen. The tool then uses the Content Posting API to upload a finished video file to that same account, with its title and hashtags, either as a direct post or as a draft for the owner to review first. It also reads the account's own public video list and follower counts so we can keep internal notes on how each release performed.

It never posts on behalf of anyone else, and it never touches any account but our own.
