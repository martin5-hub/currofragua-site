Privacy Policy

Curro Fragua auto-post — last updated 2 August 2026

Who we are

Curro Fragua auto-post is an internal desktop tool operated by the Curro Fragua music project. It is used only by the owner of the Curro Fragua accounts, to publish that project's own videos. It has no end users and is not offered to the public.

What data we handle

We handle only data belonging to our own accounts:

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens, plus the account's open_id, obtained when the account owner authorizes their own account through TikTok's OAuth screen.
  • The account's own public profile and statistics — display name, follower count, like count, video count — and the list of its own published videos. We use these to keep internal notes on how our releases performed.
  • The video files and captions we ourselves produce and upload.

We do not collect, request, receive or process data about any other TikTok user. We do not use cookies, analytics or trackers on this website.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored in a local configuration file on the operator's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server of ours, and we operate no server or database. Performance notes are kept in a private repository accessible only to the project's two members.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any of this data to third parties. The only external party involved is TikTok itself, which receives our API requests.

How long we keep it

Access tokens last about 24 hours and are refreshed automatically; refresh tokens last about 365 days. They are deleted whenever the tool is reauthorized or the configuration file is removed.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke this tool's access at any time from TikTok, under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Revoking makes the stored tokens immediately useless.
